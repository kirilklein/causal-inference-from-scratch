# Exercises: A randomized comparison

1. One randomized study estimates +17 when the simulator ATE is +12. Does that prove the assignment was not random? Explain.
2. Distinguish increasing the study size from increasing the number of simulated assignments of the same study population.
3. Why does randomization support estimation of the average effect without revealing either group's missing individual outcomes?
4. A school randomly offers tutoring, but some offered students do not attend. Does the offer contrast automatically estimate the effect of attending tutoring? State the treatment contrast randomization directly supports.
5. Run the default lab and the alternative seed in the chapter. Record the mean and SD for each population size. Explain which pattern you expect to persist across seeds and which numbers can change.
