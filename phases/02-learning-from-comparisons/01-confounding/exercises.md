# Exercises: Confounding

1. Why can treatment help every patient while treated patients have worse observed outcomes?
2. The raw estimate is −12 and the target effect is +12. Calculate the bias, including its sign. What does a smaller SD around −12 change?
3. Why does standardization use the target population's severity proportions instead of the proportions among treated patients?
4. Name the mechanism in the lab that justifies comparing treated and untreated people within severity. Why would merely recording severity in a real dataset not establish that mechanism?
5. In a new study, every high-severity patient receives treatment. Can the same nonparametric within-severity calculation estimate the effect for the full population? Explain what is missing.
6. A training program recruits workers with unusually high motivation, but motivation is not measured. Does increasing the dataset from 1,000 to 100,000 workers resolve that problem? Propose one design change that addresses assignment.
